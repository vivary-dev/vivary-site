A jar. Glass around wavy strata, a sprout growing inside, a door at the base. The vivarium as anyone keeps one: a small contained world in layers, something alive in it, and the gate the agent passes through. One ink, always the ground's text color. Never amber, never two colors, never a fill behind the glass.

Windows icon

One mark at every size, no small cut. At 16 px the jar is a jar and the sprout is a dot, and that is enough. Never a lockup below 24 px. The Windows icon is the mark in lime on a #0C100E tile, corner radius 22%, so it has its own ground on light and dark taskbars. Vivary.ico carries 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 from the one master.

Wordmark and lockups

Wordmark: the header brand word exactly. Big Shoulders 800, optical size 40, tracking +1%, as written: Vivary. Mark height 1.12 x cap height, gap 0.45 x cap. The claim under a lockup: Big Shoulders 800, opsz 72, 22% of the wordmark size, same ink.

Clear space

Keep the mark's own height clear on every side of the mark, the wordmark and every lockup, per the asset brief. The shipped SVGs carry this space inside their viewBox. Nothing enters it: no text, no rule, no screenshot edge. Minimum lockup height: 24 px mark.

The vivarium, drawn

The hero illustration for the home page: a glass cloche over four strata, ferns, stones, a door at the edge of the world, a label with one recorded line. Line art and dither in text, text-2 and text-3. Amber once. No gradients, no glow.

Keep the two palettes separate. The grounds sit 1.1:1 apart and the off-whites 1.1:1 apart, so nobody sees two blacks or two whites. Amber and lime sit at 1.6:1: same lightness, different hue, and side by side they read as warning beside success. Amber marks the record on the site. Lime is the action in the app. The mark and the wordmark are always the ground's text color. In print, text-2 #6B665C, text-3 #756F64, amber #8F5808 (5.9:1), lime #346C2B, the app's light-mode primary (6.3:1).

Do not

  • Put the mark in amber, in lime on the site, or in two colors. Use amber for anything the workspace did not record.
  • Add a second tile behind the mark, a glow, a gradient, a shadow or an outline.
  • Rotate or stretch the mark, straighten the waves, fill the glass, drop the sprout, or drop the door.
  • Reuse the old mint, navy, Bricolage Grotesque, the sparkle favicon or the glowing strata image. They belong to the retired product.
  • Set the wordmark in any face but Big Shoulders, below 700, or in all caps. Set body, nav or buttons in anything but Geist Mono.
  • Split the tagline. "It knows you." never appears without "Because you wrote it down."
  • Show a download button, a price, or a release date. The product is in development.
  • Use chat bubbles, sparkles, robots, brains, network graphs or stock photography anywhere near the brand.
